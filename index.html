GOOD TO KNOW

A few things you may wonder.

We would rather show exactly what works today than promise a feature before it is ready.

Are all fifty designs ready to purchase?＋

The fifty designs are curated visual concepts you can browse, preview and save. Three free card styles and the Palace Edit are interactive live experiences. Checkout and production ordering are still being built.

Can I add my own names and events?＋

Yes, in the live Palace Edit you can change names and up to eight ceremonies. The three free previews also let you change names and key details. Other catalog designs currently have a name preview and shortlist option.

Can I share an invitation on WhatsApp?＋

The Palace Edit can copy a personalized hosted link or open a WhatsApp share. Its link contains the invitation details and can be read by anyone you send it to. A private, short-link publishing system comes later.

Can I download a video or print a card?＋

The Palace Edit downloads a browser-recorded MP4 where supported, or WebM otherwise, plus a PNG card. Print-inspired designs are visual concepts; physical printing and print-ready files are not yet available.

Will you make animated characters of us?＋

Character animation is a bespoke service direction, separate from the static couple illustration in the Palace Edit. It needs reference photos, consent, a storyboard, likeness approval and a reviewed final render.
