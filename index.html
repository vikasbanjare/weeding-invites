08 / GOOD TO KNOW

The details,
clearly.

Are all fifty designs ready to purchase?+

All fifty open as complete, editable invitation websites. Each includes ceremony details, a cinematic playback mode, sharing and downloadable keepsakes. Checkout and production ordering are not active.

Can I edit names and ceremony details?+

Every invitation supports names, inviting families, a venue and up to eight ceremonies with individual dates, times, places and dress notes. Your edits are saved separately for each design in your browser.

Can guests RSVP?+

Add a host WhatsApp number in the editor to show a guest reply form. Guests choose ceremonies and open a prepared WhatsApp message to review and send. Replies go to the host in WhatsApp; there is no guest database or RSVP dashboard.

Can I share or download an invitation?+

Every full invitation can copy a personalized link or start a WhatsApp share. Download a PNG card, calendar file and browser-recorded MP4 where supported or WebM otherwise. Anyone with the link can read the details in it.

Do you offer printed suites or animated portraits?+

Paper and material styles are visual studies. Physical printing is not active yet. Bespoke portraits and character animation are service directions that will require references, consent and final approval.
